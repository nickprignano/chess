Privacy

This chess app is made for a family, and children may use it. It collects as little as it can.

What stays on your device

Your games, your rating, your twin, your settings and any chess.com username you type are kept in your browser on this device. They never reach our server. "Delete my data", under Menu, Your level, removes them.

chess.com

If you use "From chess.com", your browser asks chess.com directly for that player's public rating and games. chess.com sees that request, as it would if you visited their site.

The AI coach (family members only)

Signing in uses Cloudflare Access with Google or a code sent by email, and only people on the family list can sign in. Children under 13 use the coach through a parent's sign-in.

For a review or an "explain", our server sends Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, only the moves of the game, the computer's checks of them, and the coach level. Never a name, email, username or anything you typed. Anthropic doesn't train on this data, and keeps it for a limited time (about 30 days) under its policy. Reviews are labelled as written by AI.

To keep the daily limits, the server stores a counter of today's reviews under a scrambled key made from your sign-in (not your email). Counters are dropped the next day, and "Delete my data" removes yours straight away.

The idea box

Ideas you send are stored on our server for up to a year, without your name or email, for the person who builds the app to read. They are never sent to the AI.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no request logs. The site header comes from a pinned copy of the site theme on jsDelivr.
